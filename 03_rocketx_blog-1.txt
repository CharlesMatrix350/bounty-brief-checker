Title: "RocketX vs CEX: Is a Swap Aggregator Actually Better Than a Centralized Exchange?"
Type: real blog post by RocketX, condensed in my own words from screenshots. Numbers copied exactly.

LIVE TEST
- Test date: 29 June 2026. Same trade on five platforms: 0.5 BTC swapped to ETH. No promo codes or discounts.
- Results (ETH received): RocketX 18.51. SwapSpace 18.42. ChangeNOW 18.35. Swapzone 18.35. Changelly 18.16.
- Gap between best and worst: 0.35 ETH, about $362. Settlement time about two minutes.
- The post says Changelly, ChangeNOW, Swapzone and SwapSpace use their own chosen liquidity partners or their own reserves. It does not say they are CEX-only.

CLAIMS ABOUT ROCKETX
- Checks 500+ liquidity sources (DEXs, CEXs and other aggregators).
- Supports 200+ chains. Non-custodial. No account and no KYC.
- Has Privacy Swap and Private Send. Has a fixed-rate option.
- Feature table row "Best price guarantee": CEX No, DEX No, RocketX Yes.
- FAQ says quotes are snapshots, not guaranteed execution prices, and prices can move after a quote.
- Slippage on a $500,000 trade is "Moderate" for RocketX.

HONEST CAVEATS IN THE POST
- CEXs are better for fiat on-ramps and 24/7 human support. RocketX support is email and docs.
- Is RocketX always cheaper than Binance? No. High-volume Binance users with fee discounts can match or beat it on liquid pairs like BTC/USDT.
- Aggregators can lose their edge from slow RPC, paused bridges, gas spikes, vanishing liquidity and slow settlement.
